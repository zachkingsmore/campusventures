About My Ministry : Campus Ventures

Campus Ventures is a Christ-centered college ministry dedicated to helping college students glorify God through knowing and loving Him, and equipping them to be life long disciple makers of Jesus Christ.

My specific role in the ministry includes a variety of responsibilities, such as leading our worship team, discipling students, organizing community events, helping lead our bible studies, training our leadership students, and giving sermons.

Vision: Ephesians 3:7, Matthew 22:37-40, Isaiah 26:8, 2 Timothy 2:2, Acts 1:8
