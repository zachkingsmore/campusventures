About Me

My Name is Zach Kingsmore.

I am an Intern with Campus Ventures at the University of Wyoming. I am married to my beautiful wife Alayna, an elementary and special education major at the University of Wyoming.

Through my own time attending Campus Ventures, God revealed to me the importance of discipleship in reaching and changing lives, and gave me a heart for college students. Now I work for the ministry full-time as the worship director, and enjoy discipling students and helping them to know the heart of Christ for them.

In my own time, I enjoy backpacking with my wife, making music, and playing spikeball!
